Context and assumptions

Crest curves (44-3.01)

S < L: L = A S² / 2158 = K A   (Eqs. 44-3.1, 44-3.2)    S ≥ L: L = 2S − 2158 / A   (Eq. 44-3.3)

Eye height 3.5 ft, object height 2 ft. Figure 44-3A gives a minimum K (from the Figure 42-1A SSD) and a desirable K (from the SSD of the next higher speed). The truck K need not be checked. Minimum crest length is 3V ft unless existing conditions make it impractical.

Sag curves (44-3.02)

S < L: L = A S² / (400 + 3.5 S)   (Eq. 44-3.4)    S ≥ L: L = 2S − (400 + 3.5 S) / A   (Eq. 44-3.5)

Headlight height 2 ft with a 1° upward divergence. The IDM text prints "400 + 5.3 S"; the K values in Figure 44-3C are computed with 400 + 3.5 S, which this page uses. Minimum sag length is 3.2V ft. Figure 44-3C has no 15 mph row.

When S ≥ L

Where the stopping sight distance is greater than or equal to the curve length, a K value at or above the required value satisfies the criterion without the equation or graphical check (44-3.01(01) item 1). If the SSD provided exceeds that required even though K is below the table value, the K value becomes a Level Three rather than Level One design-exception item.

Decision sight distance

Figures 44-3B and 44-3D tabulate K for DSD from 20 to 65 mph. Their DSD values for 20 and 25 mph are not in Figure 42-2A, and Figure 44-3D states K = DSD²/(120 + 3.5 S); this page reports the K values exactly as printed.

Drainage

Curbed sections or concrete barrier: K ≤ 167 gives at least 0.3% grade about 50 ft from the high or low point; where K > 167, evaluate drainage near the apex or low point (flanking inlets in sags). Uncurbed: K ≤ 300 gives 0.15% at 50 ft. Avoid sag low points within the bridge shadow; desirably about 100 ft from the bridge (44-5.03(06)).
