Context and assumptions

  • Figure 49-2A gives a range of clear-zone widths for new construction or reconstruction by design speed, design-year AADT and slope. It is based on the AASHTO Roadside Design Guide curves (tangent, infinite slope length, 12-ft shoulders) and is not absolute or precise; use judgment (49-2.01). It can be used with shoulders narrower than 12 ft without adjustment. The clear zone is measured from the edge of the travel lane.
  • The IDM examples pick different points within the range (Examples 49-2.1, 49-2.3, 49-4.1 to 49-4.3); this page lets you choose the upper, midpoint or lower value.
  • Asterisked values: a wider clear zone may be provided where crash history warrants; the width may be limited to 30 ft for practicality and a consistent template where experience shows satisfactory performance.
  • No 3:1 foreslope column is tabulated: on a 3:1 foreslope recovery is less likely, fixed objects should not be present, and recovery area at the toe should be considered (Figure 49-2A note 1).
  • Curves (49-2.02(01), Figures 49-2B, 49-2C): on the outside of a curve, CZc = Kcz × CZt, interpolating on radius. Radii over 2860 ft need no adjustment per Figure 49-2B note 2 (the text of 49-2.02(01) says 3000 ft). Transition length L = 3.1V before the PC and beyond the PT. Required for new construction, reconstruction, and freeway 3R/partial 4R; otherwise a Level Two design exception. Figure 49-2B starts at 40 mph; this page uses the 40 mph column for lower speeds and flags it.
  • Slope averaging (49-2.02(02)): variable fill slopes from flat to 4:1 may be averaged within the clear zone, starting at the outside edge of the travel lane; slopes flatter than 10:1 are taken as 10:1. Slopes steeper than 4:1 are non-recoverable and are not averaged; if the clear zone extends onto them, provide a clear runout area at the toe equal to the remaining clear-zone width or 12 ft, whichever is greater (49-2.03(01)). Down-then-up ditch sections cannot be averaged (use Equations 49-2.1 to 49-2.4).
  • Low speed and curbs: collectors at 35 mph or less, local roads and streets: minimum 10 ft on tangent (49-2.03(02)). Urban facilities with vertical curbs: minimum 10 ft from the edge of travel lane or to the right-of-way line, whichever is less (49-2.03(03)); keep a 1.5-ft appurtenance-free area from the face of curb (2.5 ft for signal supports).
  • If the clear zone extends beyond the right of way, the right-of-way line is the clear-zone limit (49-2.01 item 3).
