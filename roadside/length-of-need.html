Guardrail Length of Need

Calculates the barrier length of need in advance of a hazard for adjacent traffic (Equations 49-4.2 to 49-4.4), the length for opposing traffic on a two-way roadway, and the total length LTOT = LADV + LHAZ + LOPP (Equation 49-4.1), using the runout length and shy-line offset from Figure 49-4E.

Inputs

Traffic
Adjacent traffic (approach end)
Hazard and opposing traffic

Context and assumptions

LTOT = LADV + LHAZ + LOPP    (Equation 49-4.1)
Flared: X = [LH + (b/a)(L1) − L2] / [(b/a) + (LH/LR)]   (49-4.2)    Y = LH − (LH/LR)(X)   (49-4.3)
Parallel: X = LR (LH − L2) / LH    (49-4.4)
  • X is the length of need in advance of the hazard; Y the lateral offset of the barrier at the beginning of the length of need. LH is the distance from the edge of travel lane to the back of the hazard, or the clear-zone width LC if smaller (the lesser is used, 49-4.02(01) item 1).
  • Figure 49-4E (Rev. April 2013): runout length LR by design speed and design-year AADT (≤ 1000, 1000–5000, 5000–10000, > 10000) and shy-line offset Ls. Speeds between rows (35, 65 mph) are interpolated, as in IDM Example 49-4.1.
  • The barrier should be beyond the shy-line offset where practical (49-4.01(01)). Flare rates must not exceed Figure 49-4F (see the Flare Rates page).
  • The mathematical solution applies to tangent sections only. Use the graphical solution for the outside of a horizontal curve (tangential runout path, Figure 49-4H) and for complex sites.
  • Minimum length (Figure 49-4E(1)): 50 ft with GRET type OS, MS or II; with type I, 100 ft at 50 mph or more and 50 ft at 45 mph or less.
  • Opposing traffic (49-4.02(02)): lateral dimensions from the edge of the opposing travel lane (the centerline on a 2-lane road). If needed, the length is the greater of the calculation or 100 ft (≥ 50 mph) / 50 ft (≤ 45 mph). If the barrier is beyond the opposing clear zone, no additional barrier is needed (consider a crashworthy end treatment); if the barrier is within it but the hazard is beyond it, use a crashworthy end treatment; a hazard extending well beyond may be shielded only to LC.
  • Divided highways: the trailing-end length beyond the hazard follows Figure 49-4J; avoid gaps of less than about 200 ft between runs.
  • Bridge supports ≤ 16 ft from the edge of travel lane (49-4.02(04)): round the calculated length up to the next multiple of 6.25 ft; inside the clear zone use at least 100 ft (≥ 50 mph) or 50 ft (≤ 45 mph). Guardrail configurations and pay lengths at bridges and supports follow Figures 49-4E(2) to 49-4E(10).
  • Check the barrier's dynamic deflection against the distance to the hazard (Figure 49-4A); the deflection table is shown in the results.
