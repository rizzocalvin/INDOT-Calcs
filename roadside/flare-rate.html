Barrier Flare Rates

Context and assumptions

  • Figure 49-4F gives flare rates a:b relative to the shy line: inside the shy line 13:1 (30 mph) to 30:1 (65 mph) for all barriers; outside the shy line 8:1 to 20:1 for concrete barrier and 7:1 to 15:1 for guardrail. Flatter flares are acceptable.
  • The shy-line offset Ls comes from Figure 49-4E (4.0 ft at 30 mph to 9.0 ft at 70 mph; 35 and 65 mph interpolated). The shy line is the distance beyond which a roadside object is not perceived as a threat.
  • A roadside barrier should normally be parallel to the roadway; a flared installation may be appropriate where the end is buried in the backslope (49-4.02(01)). Flaring reduces the length of need; see the Length of Need page for Equations 49-4.2 and 49-4.3.
  • Figure 49-4F stops at 65 mph; the 65 mph rates are used (and flagged) for 70 mph.
