Intersection Sight Distance

Computes the length of the sight-triangle leg along the major road for the IDM intersection control cases (Section 46-10): stop control with a left turn, right turn or crossing; yield control; left turn from the major road; and no traffic control. Time gaps include the IDM adjustments for extra lanes, medians, approach grade and skew.

Context and assumptions

ISD = 1.47 Vmajor tg    (Figure 46-10G)
  • Eye height 3.5 ft (passenger car) or 7.6 ft (truck); object height 3.5 ft (46-10.0). The ground under the sight line, not the centerline profile, governs.
  • Stop control, left turn (46-10.03(01), Figure 46-10G): check at least the passenger car and single-unit truck. INDOT uses tg = 7.5 s for a passenger car on a local road; on collectors and arterials the passenger-car gap rises from 7.5 s at 45 mph to 10.0 s at 65 to 70 mph. Add 0.5 s (car) or 0.7 s (truck) per additional lane from the left in excess of one; add 0.2 s per percent of minor-road upgrade steeper than 3% when the rear wheels are on the upgrade.
  • Right turn and crossing (Figures 46-10H, 46-10H(1)): tg = 6.5 s (car), 8.5 s (SU), 10.5 s (combination). Crossing more than two lanes adds 0.5 s (car) or 0.7 s (truck) per additional lane or narrow median; add 0.1 s per percent of upgrade steeper than 3%. The IDM tabulates the right-turn ISD for passenger cars only; this page uses the Figure 46-10H(1) gaps for trucks turning right and applies no lane adjustment to right turns.
  • Yield control (46-10.02): the minor-road leg is 80 ft (turning speed 10 mph). tg is the stop-control value plus 0.5 s: 8, 10, 12 s (Figure 46-10C); multilane left turns add 0.5/0.7 s per additional lane. Figure 46-10D gives passenger-car values to 55 mph.
  • Left turn from the major road (46-10.04, Figures 46-10I, 46-10J): tg = 5.5, 6.5, 7.5 s; add 0.5/0.7 s per additional opposing lane and for a narrow median.
  • No traffic control (46-10.01): Figure 46-10A with the Figure 46-10B grade factor; not applicable to State highways.
  • Skew (46-10.06): where the angle is less than 60°, path lengths increase. If the crossing path (lane widths ÷ sin angle) exceeds the lane widths by 12 ft or more, add lanes in the adjustment.
  • Where no adjustment applies, the tabulated (rounded) IDM value is the design value; otherwise the calculated value is shown. The IDM tables round inconsistently (e.g., 1.47 × 50 × 8.5 = 624.8 ft is printed as 630 ft).
  • If ISD cannot be provided, see the measures in 46-10.03(02) (relocate the drive, flare guardrail, revise grades, remove obstructions, etc.) and consider advance intersection warning signs with advisory speed plates.
