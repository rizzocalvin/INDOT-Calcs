Context and assumptions

IDM Section 305-3.06, Traveled Way Widening

The Green Book gives calculated and design values for traveled-way widening on open-highway curves for two-lane highways, to address off-tracking and steering difficulty. For these conditions INDOT uses full-depth shoulders on both sides of the curve in lieu of widening the traveled way. Full-depth shoulders of the calculated width should be available from the beginning of the superelevation transition before the curve to the end of the transition beyond the curve. Calculated widening of 2 ft or less can be disregarded.

Green Book Section 3.3.10 equations (not reproduced in the IDM)

w = Wc − Wn    Wc = N(U + C) + (N − 1) FA + Z
U = u + R − √(R² − ΣLi²)    FA = √(R² + A(2L + A)) − R    Z = 0.1 V / √R

C is the lateral clearance per vehicle: 2.0, 2.5 and 3.0 ft for tangent traveled-way widths of 20, 22 and 24 ft. These equations and the clearance values come from the Green Book, not from the IDM; verify them and the design-vehicle dimensions against your edition of the Green Book. Rounding of Green Book design values is not applied.

Wider turning roadways at intersections are a separate topic (IDM Figure 46-3B, derived pavement widths for turning roadways).
