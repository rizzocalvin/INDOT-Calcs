Context and assumptions

  • 4R (Chapter 53): new construction or reconstruction. The Green Book minimum values may be used where lower than the Chapter 53 values, except where they would supersede State or Federal requirements (e.g., 12-ft lanes on the National Truck Network; ADA/PROWAG). Chapter 53 is being rewritten as Chapter 302.
  • 3R (Chapter 55): resurfacing, restoration and rehabilitation of existing non-freeways. Chapter 54 covers freeway 3R and partial 4R; Chapter 56 covers partial 3R projects (pavement-width tables and work-type matrices).
  • Rows marked with an asterisk (*) are controlling criteria; values below them need a design exception (Section 40-8.0).
  • Usable shoulder width is the width usable for emergency stopping; the effective usable width is the usable width minus 1 ft, but not less than the paved width (45-1.02(01)). Auxiliary lanes should match the adjacent through lane width, but not be less than 1 ft narrower (45-1.03).
  • Values are shown exactly as printed in the IDM figure, including Des./Min. notation. The width check uses the parsed minimum and desirable values; cells that are not plain widths are reported but not checked.
  • Where shoulders are near guardrail, see Section 49-5.0. Footnotes are critical to correct use of the figures (53-1.0 item 8).
