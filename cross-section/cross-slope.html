Cross Slopes (Tangent Sections)

Gives the IDM travel-lane, auxiliary-lane and shoulder cross slopes for a tangent section on a State highway, based on the number of lanes, crown location, shoulder width and underdrains (Sections 45-1.01(02), 45-1.02(05), 45-1.03; Figures 45-1A(1) and 45-1A(2)).

Inputs

Roadway
Outside edge

Results

Diagram

One direction of travel, from the crown or median edge outward. Arrows point downslope. Slopes exaggerated.

Context and assumptions

Travel lanes (45-1.01(02))

  • 2-lane highway: crowned at the centerline, 2% away from the center.
  • Divided, two lanes each direction: each roadway crowned at its centerline, 2%.
  • Three lanes, new or reconstruction: crown on the lane line between the center lane and the median-side lane; the right two lanes slope to the outside; all at 2%.
  • Three lanes added to an existing facility: keep the existing crown; added lanes match the adjacent lane. Where three lanes slope the same direction, the third lane is 3%.
  • Four lanes: crowned at the one-way roadway centerline (two lanes each side) at 2%. Where three or more lanes slope the same direction, the third and fourth lanes are 3%.
  • Existing roadway with two lanes sloped one direction: thicken the overlay 3 in. at the inside edge for a uniform 2%; with three lanes, use 3% on the outside lane; a lane added in the median slopes 2% toward the median.
  • Bridges: 2% from the crown to the face of rail or curb; an existing bridge to remain may keep 1.5%. Non-State paved: 2%; non-State aggregate: 6%.

Shoulders (45-1.02(05), Figures 45-1A(1), 45-1A(2))

  • Paved shoulder 4 ft or narrower: same slope as the adjacent travel lane (2%).
  • Paved shoulder over 4 ft with underdrains: 2% for the 2 ft next to the travel lane, then 4%. Without underdrains: 4%.
  • Curb offset: paved, same slope as the adjacent lane (typically 2%). Aggregate shoulders 4 to 6%; earth shoulders 6 to 8%.
  • Where the travel-lane slope differs from 2%, a 4-ft-or-narrower shoulder matches the lane slope.

Auxiliary lanes (45-1.03)

Cross slope 1% greater than the adjacent through lane. For superelevated sections see the Superelevation page and Section 305-4.06 (shoulders) and 305-4.07 (auxiliary lanes).
