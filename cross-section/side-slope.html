Context and assumptions

Fill slopes (45-3.01, Figures 45-3A and 45-3B)

  • New construction: 6:1 to the edge of the clear zone; if the slope has not reached natural ground there, 3:1 or flatter to the toe (freeways, arterials, collectors).
  • Rural local roads: 4:1 desirable, 3:1 maximum for fills up to 30 ft; 3:1 maximum over 30 ft. Urban local streets: 3:1 maximum.
  • Curbed facilities (Figure 45-3B): the fill starts X beyond the curb on a 12:1 slope; X = 5 ft rural (11 ft with sidewalk), 11 ft urban (5 ft without sidewalk). Urban arterials, collectors and local streets: 3:1 maximum.
  • Slopes in Figures 45-3A and 45-3B are for new construction; see Chapter 49 for reconstruction.

Cut slopes (45-3.02, Figures 45-3C and 45-3D)

  • Freeway: 6:1 foreslope; backslope 4:1 for 20 ft, then 4:1 (H ≤ 10 ft) or 3:1 (H > 10 ft).
  • Arterial or rural collector: 6:1 foreslope; backslope 4:1 for 20 ft, then 3:1 maximum to top.
  • Urban collector: foreslope 6:1 desirable, 4:1 maximum; backslope 4:1 for 4 ft, then 3:1 maximum.
  • Rural local road: 4:1 maximum foreslope and backslope for V ≥ 50 mph; 3:1 for V ≤ 45 mph. Urban local street: 3:1 maximum.
  • Curbed cut (Figure 45-3D): 11-ft shelf (5 ft without sidewalk); backslope 4:1 maximum rural, 4:1 urban arterial/collector, 3:1 urban local.
  • Ditch grade: 1% or steeper needs sod; 3% or steeper needs paved or riprap lining; flattest unpaved ditch grade 0.5% (0.3% restricted).

2:1 slopes (45-3.03)

Avoid slopes of 2:1 or steeper on INDOT projects unless absolutely necessary. On a 4R project a 2:1 sideslope requires Level Two design-exception documentation at the grade-review stage. On 3R projects consider 3:1, then 2.5:1 before 2:1. In rock cuts use 3:1 rather than 2:1 unless good stability or sound rock is verified. Chapter 55 3R figures list their own side-slope values; see the Lane & Shoulder Widths page.

Recoverability (Chapter 49)

Fill slopes 4:1 or flatter can be slope-averaged within the clear zone. Slopes steeper than 4:1 are non-recoverable; if the clear zone extends onto them, provide a clear runout area at the toe equal to the clear-zone width remaining on the slope or 12 ft, whichever is greater (49-2.03(01)). A 3:1 foreslope is not tabulated in Figure 49-2A; fixed objects should not be present on it.
